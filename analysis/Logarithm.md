
"$log$" gives back the power we need to raise number $b$ with to, to get number $x$ - $\log_b(x)$ 

$\log_2(10) = 3.3219280948873626$; 
$2^{3.3219280948873626} ≈ 10$  

The "base" of $log$ is $e=2.718281828459045...$
